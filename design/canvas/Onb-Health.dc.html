Quick health check

Five yes/no questions. Answers stay on your phone.

1Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition or high blood pressure?
2Do you get chest pain at rest, in daily life, or when you exercise?
3Have you lost balance from dizziness, or passed out, in the last 12 months?
4Are you being treated for any other long-term medical condition?
5Do you have a bone, joint or muscle problem that exercise could make worse?
