Design system · data

Charts and tables.

Quiet grids, thin marks, numbers in Azeret Mono. The chart palette is validated for colour-blind separation on both surfaces (worst CVD ΔE 9.4 dark / 9.2 light). The light aqua is under 3:1, so the macro bar is always labelled.

  • 01One series? Use lume and no legend; the title names it. Two or more get a legend and direct labels.
  • 02Series colours (1–3) are only for categories like macros. They never mean good or bad; semantic colours do that, always with a label.
  • 03One y-axis, ever. Weight and strength sit in two charts, stacked, never on a dual axis.
  • 04Every chart has a table view one tap away. That’s the accessible version and the export.
  • 05Show estimates as estimates: e1RM says “estimated”, food says “imported”.
