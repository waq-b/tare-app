How to
  1. 1Lie with eyes under the bar, feet flat, shoulder blades pulled back and down.
  2. 2Grip just wider than shoulders. Unrack with straight arms.
  3. 3Lower to mid-chest under control, elbows about 45° from your body.
  4. 4Press up and slightly back to over your shoulders.
