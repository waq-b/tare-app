Five names. Tare it is.

Zeroing the scale: an honest reading with nothing added. The other four stay here for the record. Wordmarks are quick sketches in the app’s own type system, not finished logos.

A plumb line shows what’s true, not what you hoped. Fits the honest coach. Slightly harder to say out loud.

Notch

One notch at a time: small, finishable progress, which is the miniquest idea itself. Punchier than the brief’s mood, though.

Tally

Marks on a wall. Every set counts, quietly. Warm and obvious, but a common word, so harder to own.
